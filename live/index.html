Lineage (78-gene developmental connectome)

All innate motor, whole-body synergy, gaze, roll-damping and sense→MOT steering pathways grow from a 78-gene developmental genome. "Evolve" runs a variance-reduced Mirrored Separable CMA-ES in background Web Workers: 8 antithetic mutants per generation are scored on a paired 2-body rough-ground + steering Common-Random-Number cohort, with elitist mean verification so lineage fitness improves monotonically on the validation cohort. "Adopt" grows a new creature from the evolved genome; "Previous genome" steps back one adoption.
