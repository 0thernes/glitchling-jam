- **No physical quantum coherence or non-locality** is claimed: all quantum modules (`FMO`, `Cry4`, `CHSH`, `CEGA-18`, `Hoggar`, `C3`) execute classical IEEE-754 / exact rational linear algebra on CPU (with optional WASM/WebGPU).
