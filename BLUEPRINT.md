# Glitchling v2 — BLUEPRINT

*Design spec, written Mon Nov 2 2026, 05:30 PHT (UTC+8). Status key: **REAL** = in code and running today; **PLANNED** = specified here, not built; **SIMULATED** = an in-sim mechanic that plays a fictional role (psychic, remote viewing). It is not a claim about real phenomena.*

Glitchling is Creature #1's deliberately broken baby. v1/v2 today is `glitchling.ts` (54 lines) subclassing `core/organism.ts`. That gives 272 neurons in 14 regions, a full metabolism, a 6-layer affect system and a developmental genome, plus 4 switchable flaws. v2's goal is Alex's: **nothing cosmetic**. Every visible thing is a readout of sim state, and every state variable is driven by, and drives, at least two other areas.

Grounding: this spec reuses three sources:
- the jam's own copy of Creature #1 (`core/`);
- the CQM box copy (`cqm-copy/src`, read-only, nothing modified);
- the existing three.js studio (`cqm-copy/src/sim/glitchling/studio-v3.ts`) and its 4K render (`artifacts/artificial-life/hdr/`).

Of the 100 areas, **76 map to existing code** and **24 are new** (column "Reuse" in §1; also `cqm_reuse`/`status` in `data/areas.json`). Of the 500 attributes, **106 name a variable that already exists** (✓). The other 394 are planned names.

## 0. Honest starting point (what CQM actually gives us)
Several CQM modules carry their own `Audit #… (honesty, documented)` headers. These matter because "reuse" often means *port the structure, then train or wire it for real*:
- `sim/super-creature.ts`: cortex/actor weights random, never trained.
- `sim/xenomimic-brain.ts`: 100-param MLP is predict-only. `mimicBasin` is ≡0.5, so broadcast = 0.
- `sim/theory-of-mind.ts`: ToM MLP untrained, never checked against the rival's real plan.
- `sim/metacognition.ts`: self-model untrained.
- `sim/morphic-field.ts`: latent imprint is ≤0.0012/beat. Its only live effect is a chaos nudge.
- `sim/topdown-perception.ts`: percept offset near-constant.

Solid, reusable-as-is: `math/izhikevich.ts` (real spiking dynamics), `math/predictive-coding.ts`, `math/global-workspace.ts`, `sim/genome.ts` (Creatures-style genetics), `sim/lineage.ts`, `sim/mortality.ts`, `sim/weather.ts`, `sim/terrain-deformation.ts`, `sim/edible-resource.ts`, `sim/social-field.ts` (SoA neighbourhood kernel + `workers/social-field-worker.ts`), `sim/stigmergy.ts`, `math/spatial-hash.ts`, `core/wilderness-webgpu.ts` (batched FP32 WebGPU kernel: the template for GPU scaling). From the jam core, `core/organism.ts`, `core/affect.ts` and `core/develop.ts` are the real, measured backbone.

**Rule for v2:** a CQM module enters Glitchling only after it gets (a) a live input from the organism's state and (b) an output another area consumes. A unit test must show the output changes when the input changes. That is the "nothing cosmetic" gate. It would have caught each audit note above.

- **Central brain** (REAL, 272 neurons): `core/organism.ts` REGIONS SENS…ENS. v2 adds spiking dynamics via `math/izhikevich.ts`, a predictive layer from `math/predictive-coding.ts`, and the workspace from `math/global-workspace.ts`. Wiring is grown by `core/develop.ts`, not hand-set.
- **Limb brains** (PLANNED): each leg/arm gets a ganglion `lb[j]` with 32–256 neurons, a CPG oscillator (phase → `legs[j].phase`) and a local reflex arc (nociception → retract within 1 tick). The central brain sends low-bandwidth goals (heading, reach target). The limb sends contact/proprioception summaries. Legs can therefore keep walking, or fight the centre, when the uplink is scrambled. That makes them weird and partly autonomous by construction. Structure taken from `sim/entity-brain.ts` (per-entity small controllers).
- **Wireless field** (SIMULATED): a 2-D lattice `wf` (reuse `sim/social-field.ts` SoA kernel, `sim/mind-field.ts`, `sim/stigmergy.ts` decay/diffusion). Each creature writes `wf.amp·payload` (an 8-dim vector made from affect + workspace content) at its position with falloff. Every creature reads the local field as an extra sensory modality. **Remote viewing** = a costly action (`rv.atp`, takes ATP and sleep pressure) that samples `rv.sample[]` (food/threat at a distant cell) with fidelity < 1 and a false-image rate. These are ordinary sim mechanics with real costs and real errors, labelled SIMULATED everywhere they show up in UI and exports.
- **Body-morph** (PLANNED; render-side exists in `sim/super-body.ts`, `sim/nhi-body.ts`, `creature-exterior-*.ts`): body = spine of `nSeg` segments with radius, hydrostatic pressure and fluid volume. Expand/contract/extend/shorten are motor outputs that change `pressure`/`len`, cost ATP (`morph.atpCost`), and change sim physics: reach, speed, predator detection, heat loss via surface area. Mimicry/camouflage set `morph.target` and `skin.rgb` toward a sampled model, scored by `camo.pDetect` from predator vision. Limb and eye counts are genome-set and odd counts are allowed. Eyes can sit on legs.
- **Metabolism** (REAL): `core/organism.ts` gut/blood/liverGly/fat/muscle/atp/heat. v2 charges every action (morph, signal, remote view, neural spikes via `cogCost`) to ATP. Starvation, heat stress and hypoxia feed affect and death.
- **Reproduction & genome** (PARTLY REAL): `devGenome` + `genes` + `Affect.birth` (heritable persona with bet-hedging) exist. v2 adds Creatures-style genome ops from `sim/genome.ts` and the kin tree from `sim/lineage.ts`. It also adds an energy-gated procreation event that spawns a child organism with recombined, mutated genome, plus nurture actions (feed/warm/guard) that transfer the parent's ATP and set bond weights. Selection is implicit: whoever leaves descendants.
- **Affect** (REAL): `core/affect.ts`: fear/delight/frustration/calm → mood → attitude → personality, plus HPA lock-in, sleep debt, sickness, incentive sensitisation. v2 adds attachment/grief (48), social contagion through the field (41, 62), and a wellbeing log (50). - **Environment** (REUSE): `sim/weather.ts`, `sim/terrain-deformation.ts`/`terrain-profile.ts`/`biome-strata.ts`, `sim/edible-resource.ts`, `sim/vegetation.ts`, `sim/waste-ecology.ts` (excretion fertilises), `sim/dome-feeding.ts`. ### Scaling path (honest compute)
Box today: 8 CPU cores, 15 GB RAM (~5–7 GB free, shared), **no GPU**. Today's tick costs ~3 ms for 272 neurons (from the AL headless log).

| tier | neurons | synapses (= params) | memory (CSR f32 + idx) | where | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T0 today | 2.7e2 | ~1e4 | <1 MB | CPU | REAL. `core/organism.ts` |
| T1 | 1e3–1e4 | 1e5–1e6 | ≤10 MB | CPU, one thread | central + 10 limb brains; Phase 1–2 |
| T2 | 1e5 | 1e7 | ~100 MB | CPU, workers (`workers/`) | about fly-scale neuron count (FlyWire ≈1.4e5 neurons, ≈5e7 synapses) |
| T3 | 1e6 | 1e8 | ~1.2 GB (w 0.4 + idx 0.4 + trace 0.4) | CPU slow / GPU | **100M-synapse / 100M-param target lives here** |
| T4 | 1e8 | 1e8–1e10 | 2.5 GB (fanout 1) … 100+ GB (fanout 100) | GPU cluster | the literal "1e8 nodes" |

Candid points:
- **"100M neurons + 100M synapses" is internally odd.** It means about one synapse per neuron, while biology has ~10²–10⁴. The meaningful target is **1e8 synapses ≈ 1e8 parameters on ~1e6 neurons (T3)**. That is roughly 2× the fly connectome's synapse count. Out-counting the fly does **not** mean out-performing it: the fly's wiring is evolved and functional, and ours is grown from a toy genome.
- **CPU (this box):** T3 fits in RAM. Use event-driven sparse updates: only spiking neurons push along CSR rows. At ~1% activity that is ~1e6 synaptic events per substep, roughly 10–50 ms per substep on 8 cores in tight typed-array JS/WASM. Since 1 tick = 1 sim-minute and is not real-time, a 1-day run is hours, not minutes. Fine for offline experiments; too slow for interactive render. T4 does not fit (neuron state alone is 0.8 GB, and dense stepping 1e8 neurons is ~1e11 updates/s for real time).
- **GPU:** T3 runs interactively on one consumer GPU (24 GB) using the batched-FP32 pattern in `core/wilderness-webgpu.ts`. Plasticity, though, is memory-bandwidth bound. T4 needs multi-GPU partitioning by region plus spike exchange, and is out of jam scope.
- **Techniques:** event-driven spiking (Izhikevich/LIF), CSR per region, f16/int8 weights (`math/quantization.ts`), local plasticity only (no backprop through 1e8). Limb brains run in their own worker and talk via message passing, which is also how the "wireless" uplink gets latency and loss. Grow wiring with `core/develop.ts` rules rather than storing random matrices.
- **What scaling buys:** nothing, until the task demands it. More neurons on a 16-site forage task changes little. Scale the brain together with sensor richness (retina[], touch map[]) and body DOF, so that capacity is used.
