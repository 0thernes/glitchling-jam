    What's real vs simplified

    KEPT FROM THE REFERENCE

    • 16 food sites = 4 colours × 4 shapes; reward probability 0.05 + 0.25·cv[c]/3 + 0.25·sv[s]/3, with sites depleting after a meal and recovering over 120 ticks.
    • Only half the sites (a Latin pattern) are reachable on days 1–3. 1 tick = 1 sim-minute; awake 06:00–22:00.
    • The brain layout: 272 neurons in the same 14 regions and sizes (SENS 16, THAL 16, CTX 64, HIP 32, STR 32, HYP 16, VTA/RAPHE/LC/BF 6, INS 8, SCN 8, MOT 8, ENS 48), wired with the reference's region-to-region connection probabilities.
    • The four flaws, applied the way glitchling.ts applies them: permuted + noisy site senses re-shuffled every N ticks; 6%/tick decay of learned values toward 0.25; reversed MOT readout plus mirrored site choice; periodic jolts to one site value and 20 synapses. Seeded mulberry32 RNG, separate flaw stream.

    SIMPLIFIED ON THIS 2D PAGE

    • Neurons here are rate units. Real in live/: 3,268 spiking leaky integrate-and-fire neurons and ~123,000 synapses by default (436–25,924 across S/M/L/XL, optional Web Worker and WebGPU backends) across the same 14 regions plus proprioceptive and motor-neuron pools, DA/NE/5-HT/ACh read from VTA/LC/raphe/basal-forebrain firing plus cortisol from the body, STDP eligibility traces turned into weight change by dopamine with three-mechanism homeostasis, and WAKE/N1/N2/N3/REM sleep with NREM replay and REM dreaming.
    • Metabolism here is 3 pools. Real in live/: gut → blood → liver glycogen → fat, ketones, gluconeogenesis, urea, muscle protein, insulin/glucagon (PID), cortisol, ghrelin, leptin, ATP from oxidation (rate laws ported from core/organism.ts), plus water, bladder, colon and waste.
    • The decision here is a noisy race over values. Real in live/: a unified spike race between 21 D1 and 21 D2 striatal neurons (16 plants, 3 water pools, dropped food and epistemic exploration) driven by expected free energy (value, Beta-belief information gain, travel cost, learned harm), a metacognitive veto that can cancel the winner, and REM dreaming that writes beliefs and runs deep world-model rollouts.
    • On this page MOT encodes an 8-way heading on a 2D map. In live/: a 78-gene multi-body CMA-ES developmental genome grows the sense→MOT and CPG/MOT→muscle wiring so the 8 MOT neurons drive an LTC pattern generator and 122 motor neurons that pull 61 muscle channels of a 3D soft body; which goal to visit comes from the striatal race.
    • Both pages are simulations running in your browser. Neither makes any claim of awareness, feelings, sentience or consciousness.
