# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1 Summary)

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the `glitchling-jam` community a harassment-free, intellectually rigorous, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Standards
- Focus critique on code, proofs, statistical methodology, and falsifiable claims — never on individuals.
- Maintain strict epistemic honesty: distinguish verified facts, simulation artifacts, and open questions clearly.
- Respect reproducible-science norms: cite data, seeds, versions, and error bounds accurately.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, doxxing, or bad-faith disruption.
- Fabrication of data, citations, benchmarks, or formal proof outputs.

## Enforcement
Report issues directly to the maintainer at `0_0@0thernes.art`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
