# Support

## Getting Help
- **Usage & Quickstart:** See [`README.md`](README.md), [`REPLICATION.md`](REPLICATION.md), and [`docs/faq.md`](docs/faq.md).
- **Bug Reports & Falsification Challenges:** Open an issue using the structured templates in [`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/), tagging the appropriate finding class (`bug`, `error`, `issue`, `problem`, `concern`, `dilemma`, `conflict`, `gap`, `hole`, `blindspot`).
- **Security Vulnerabilities:** Do not open a public issue; follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) (`0_0@0thernes.art`).
- **Academic & Replication Inquiries:** Contact Alexander Stephen Donahue (`0_0@0thernes.art`, ORCID `0009-0006-8168-9648`).

## Scope & Support Expectations
This is an independent open-science research repository maintained on a best-effort basis (`Status: active`). Pull requests are not required; reproducible bug reports, counterexamples, and independent replication logs (see [`REPLICATION.md`](REPLICATION.md)) are warmly welcomed.
