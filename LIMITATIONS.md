## 2. Known Technical Limitations
1. **Bun/JSC Cross-Machine Nondeterminism (`F31`):** Under default Bun settings, JSC concurrent JIT timing makes `glitchling.ts` nondeterministic. Setting `BUN_JSC_useConcurrentJIT=0` guarantees same-machine bit-identical repeatability, but cross-machine floating-point/JSC differences can still produce slightly different realizations for seeds `500–501` (detected and reported honestly by `tools/check.mjs`).
2. **Cosmogonic Non-Commercial License (`F04/D4`):** `LICENSE` is a non-commercial research-and-play license (identical to `0thernes/CQM2`), not an OSI-approved open-source license; commercial use requires explicit written permission. See [`docs/adr/0001-cosmogonic-license.md`](docs/adr/0001-cosmogonic-license.md).
3. **Mega-Atlas Open Problems (`0 / 1,900` Solved):** Cross-checking all 1,900 problems in `0thernes/unsolved-problems-mega-atlas` confirms Glitchling solves **zero** open problems in mathematics, physics, biology, or AI; it offers a reproducible discrete testbed touching scoped aspects of `AI-045`, `BIO-022`, `BIO-026`, `BIO-044`, `QM-007`, `QM-011`, and `STAT-060`.
4. **AI-Assisted Engineering Disclosure:** Code hardening, Lean 4 formalization, Python differential oracles, and manuscript drafting used AI coding and research assistants under direct human oversight. The theorems in `lean/` are checked by Lean 4.35.0 and the numerical claims were re-executed from live code; the remaining mathematical statements in the paper rest on the Python oracles or on cited literature.

## 3. Scope of the Lean Statements (statement audit, 2026-11-26)

The following Lean declarations are kernel-checked but prove less than their names or earlier descriptions suggested. Details and corrections are in [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md); audit log: <https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md>.

1. `rs15_11_mds_radius` checks only `d = 15 - 11 + 1 = 5` and `t = (d - 1) / 2 = 2`. No Reed–Solomon code, minimum distance or decoder is defined in Lean; decoding correctness rests on property tests.
2. `c3_cubic_trace_gap` checks only `15360 - 14976 = 384` and `384 * 40 = 15360` on literals. The traces $\operatorname{Tr}\Lambda^3$ are computed by the exact-rational Python oracle, not in Lean.
3. `bell_diagonal_exact_no_signaling` is definitional: the off-diagonal marginal is defined as `0` and the diagonal marginal as `a + b`. No 4×4 matrix, partial trace or measurement basis is modelled.
4. `clausius_entropy_nonneg` assumes non-negative fluxes `qDiss, qGeo, qRad >= 0` as well as `0 < T_sky <= T_amb <= T_geo`, and concerns a hand-written numerator; it does not derive the Second Law.
5. The N/C mass ledger has single-step preservation lemmas (`fluxSoil_preserves_mass`, `transferSoil_preserves_mass`); only the energy ledger has a theorem over every finite operation sequence.
6. The GF(16) results do not state additive associativity or the additive identity (both hold for XOR but are not kernel-checked).
7. `nestor_sr_unbiased` and `nestor_sr_variance_bound` are integer polynomial identities with no probability model; `poincare_mobius_preserves_disk` covers rational (integer-scaled) points of the open unit disk, not real or floating-point inputs.
8. `heptarch_anti_duopoly` shows that any two seats hold at most 92 of 100 and that a third seat has positive weight; two seats can still hold a majority.
9. The 6-kernel `lake comparator` run checks the 9 comparator targets and their dependency closure, not every declaration separately.
