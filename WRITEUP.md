# Glitchling

Glitchling is Creature #1's baby sibling, built broken on purpose. Every 90 ticks its senses get rewired, so whatever it learned about the world stops lining up with what it feels. Its memory leaks 6% every tick, all eight of its motors run backwards, and its mutations misfire. It survived anyway. In Results v3 (30 seeds per setup, 2 sim-days each, re-run on 2026-11-24 in a repeatable mode), all 30 creatures with every flaw on were still alive at the end. They hit fewer of the things they went for (success 0.30 versus 0.37 for the healthy clone; lower in 26 of 30 seeds) and made up for it by foraging about 22% more (255 versus 209 forages, 95% CI for the paired difference +35 to +57). So they ate about as often (76 meals versus 75) with slightly higher glucose (4.03 versus 3.95). The flaw that matters most is the mirrored site choice ("backwards"); misfiring mutations alone did nothing measurable. An earlier two-seed run (Results v2) hinted that the broken creature's hit rate improved within a life. With 30 seeds that hint did not hold up (−0.016, 95% CI −0.042 to +0.009). This is a toy simulation from a one-day jam, so the numbers describe this model, not real animals. Still, it's a nice result: break every part of a creature and it can still find a way to eat, by trying more often.
